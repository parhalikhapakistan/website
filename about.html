About PLP

Turning knowledge into opportunity for people and organizations

Parha Likha Pakistan (PLP) is a Professional Training & Consulting Company. We help individuals build practical skills, help organizations adopt AI and digital technology with confidence, and work to widen access to learning for those who need it most.

Who we are

Practical learning, connected to real opportunity

PLP brings together professional development and educational empowerment. We serve individual learners and corporate organizations through hands-on training, technology education, AI consulting and digital solutions.

Our approach is simple: keep learning practical, keep technology purposeful, and keep people at the centre. Every program uses real-world scenarios, live demonstrations and reusable workflows that participants can apply the very next day.

Training is delivered online and onsite, in English or Urdu, and adapted to each audience's role, experience and goals.

Our Mission

To make quality education, professional training and practical AI and digital skills accessible to all; empower individuals to grow with confidence; and help organizations learn, adopt and build with technology responsibly to strengthen their people and performance.

Our Vision

A skilled and empowered Pakistan where every person and organization can learn, grow and thrive through knowledge and technology.

Our objectives

What we set out to do

  • Deliver practical, relevant and high-quality professional training.
  • Support organizations through corporate learning, technology education, consulting and digital solutions.
  • Expand access to learning through scholarships, subsidized programs and community initiatives.
  • Build digital literacy and confidence among learners with different levels of experience.
  • Promote responsible, inclusive and purposeful use of emerging technologies.
  • Create learning pathways that connect skills development with career, business and community opportunities.
  • Continuously review programs to keep them relevant to learner and employer needs.
