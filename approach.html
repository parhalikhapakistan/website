Our Approach

Practical frameworks for using AI with confidence

PLP's training, consulting and solutions are built on a small set of clear, repeatable frameworks. They help people use AI tools productively, protect information and keep humans accountable for important decisions.

Framework 1

The PLP Practical AI Workflow

A seven-step routine for turning everyday tasks into reliable, AI-assisted work, from choosing the right task to measuring the result.

Every output is checked by a responsible person before it is used.

  1. Identify the taskDefine the work and the outcome you need.
  2. Prepare the informationGather inputs and remove unnecessary sensitive data.
  3. Select the AI toolChoose a suitable, organization-approved tool.
  4. Write the promptGive context, input, instructions and output requirements.
  5. Generate and refineReview the response and improve it step by step.
  6. Verify and approveCheck facts, calculations, sources, tone and risks.
  7. Apply and measureUse the approved result and track its usefulness.

Framework 2

The PLP 7-Part Professional Prompt

A structure that turns vague requests into clear, professional instructions that produce useful results the first time.

  • 1 Role
  • 2 Context
  • 3 Objective
  • 4 Input
  • 5 Instructions
  • 6 Output
  • 7 Restrictions
Weak prompt

Write an email to the client about the delay.

Structured prompt

ROLE · CONTEXT · OBJECTIVE · OUTPUT · RESTRICTIONSAct as a client-service manager. Our project delivery is delayed by one week because of pending approvals. Write a short, professional email that informs the client, explains the reason and confirms the new date. Keep it under 150 words, polite and clear. Do not promise anything not stated above.

Framework 3

The PLP AI Adoption Framework

For organizations, adoption works best as a controlled journey: start small, prove value, then scale with the right training and guidance.

We also use a simple opportunity prioritization score (1–5) across business impact, time savings, frequency, feasibility, data readiness and risk to decide what to pilot first.

  1. DiscoverUnderstand goals, teams, tasks and constraints.
  2. AssessReview readiness across people, processes, technology and governance.
  3. PrioritizeShortlist the highest-value, lowest-risk opportunities.
  4. EnableTrain teams and provide prompts, workflows and tools.
  5. PilotTest in a controlled way with clear success measures.
  6. EvaluateReview adoption, feedback and value indicators.
  7. ImproveRefine, expand and repeat.

Framework 4

PLP Responsible AI Principles

Seven principles that apply to every program, engagement and solution we deliver.

Purposeful use

Use AI where it genuinely helps the task and the people involved.

Privacy first

Protect personal and confidential information at every step.

Accuracy & verification

Check facts, figures and sources before outputs are used.

Fairness & inclusion

Watch for bias and design for people of different backgrounds.

Human accountability

People remain responsible for consequential decisions.

Transparency

Be open about when and how AI is used.

Continuous improvement

Review results and update practices as tools change.
