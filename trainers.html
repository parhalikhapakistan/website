Lead Trainer – AI, Excel, Power BI & Data Analytics

Jahangir Sachwani

AI Transformation & BI Consultant · Corporate Trainer

Jahangir Sachwani is a business intelligence consultant, corporate trainer and technology professional who has worked in training and development since 1998. He holds a Master's degree in Computer Science and has trained more than 500,000 professionals for over 250 corporate clients.

He is the Founder and CEO of Innovinc AI and the Founder and Director of T Team (SMC-Pvt) Limited, a Microsoft Power BI reporting company. Through this work he applies his experience in analytics, reporting and workforce development to AI, helping organizations improve workflows, reporting, decision-making and productivity.

He has delivered face-to-face programs in Pakistan and the UAE, and live online training for audiences in Australia, Bangladesh, Belgium, Canada, China, the GCC, the Maldives, Mauritius, Singapore, Sri Lanka and the USA, in English and Urdu.
