AI-01

Generative AI for Business Professionals

Build a solid, practical foundation in generative AI: what it can and cannot do, how to prompt professionally, and how to apply it safely to everyday business work.

Enquire
  • 12 hours / 6 sessions
  • Business professionals, managers, entrepreneurs and individual learners
  • Online or onsite
  • English or Urdu
Session 1Introduction to Generative AI
  • What generative AI is and how it differs from traditional software
  • Common capabilities and limitations of text, image and presentation tools
  • Examples of AI use in everyday professional work
  • Human judgment, verification and responsible use

Practical exercise: Identify suitable and unsuitable tasks for generative AI.

Session 2Working with AI Assistants
  • Practical orientation to ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude
  • Writing clear instructions and providing context
  • Iterative prompting and refining responses
  • Choosing tools according to task requirements

Practical exercise: Draft, revise and compare a professional communication.

Session 3Professional Prompting
  • Role, context, objective, input, instructions, output and restrictions
  • Weak versus structured prompts
  • Output formats, examples and constraints
  • Prompt refinement and reusable templates

Practical exercise: Convert vague requests into structured professional prompts.

Session 4Business Applications
  • Research and information synthesis
  • Email, report and document drafting
  • Meeting preparation and summaries
  • Idea generation and planning support

Practical exercise: Complete a business task and verify the result.

Session 5Productivity and Integrated Workflows
  • Combining AI tools with office tasks
  • Creating repeatable workflows
  • Using source documents and reference material
  • Quality control and human approval

Practical exercise: Design a repeatable workflow for a workplace task.

Session 6Responsible AI and Practical Project
  • Privacy and confidential information
  • Hallucinations, bias and verification
  • Copyright and transparency awareness
  • Practical project presentation

Practical exercise: Present an AI-assisted task with appropriate safeguards.

AI-02

ChatGPT, Google Gemini & Claude – Practical Applications

Get hands-on with the three leading AI assistants. Learn which tool fits which task, and how to research, write, analyze and work with documents more effectively.

Enquire
  • 12 hours / 6 sessions
  • Professionals and teams
  • Online or onsite
  • English or Urdu
Session 1Tool Orientation and Comparison
  • Capabilities of ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude
  • Interface and account considerations
  • Task-based tool selection
  • Feature availability and plan differences

Practical exercise: Run the same low-risk task through available assistants and compare responses.

Session 2Research and Information Synthesis
  • Question framing and context
  • Summarizing supplied information
  • Comparing viewpoints and identifying gaps
  • Checking sources and claims

Practical exercise: Prepare a short research brief from supplied non-confidential sources.

Session 3Writing and Communication
  • Professional emails and letters
  • Reports, proposals and summaries
  • Tone, audience and format control
  • Editing and iterative refinement

Practical exercise: Create a professional communication package.

Session 4Analysis and Problem Solving
  • Structuring a problem
  • Working with tables and supplied data
  • Generating options and comparing trade-offs
  • Checking calculations and assumptions

Practical exercise: Analyze a sample business scenario and validate its recommendations.

Session 5Documents and Knowledge Work
  • Working with user-provided documents where supported
  • Extracting themes and action items
  • Creating FAQs and structured knowledge summaries
  • Source-grounded responses and limitations

Practical exercise: Create a reference-based FAQ from approved sample material.

Session 6Integrated Practical Challenge
  • Select the right assistant for a task
  • Compare outputs and document differences
  • Improve prompts and refine final deliverables
  • Responsible use and final review

Practical exercise: Complete and present a multi-step business task.

Enquire
  • 8 hours / 4 sessions
  • Corporate leadership, managers and cross-functional teams
  • Online or onsite
  • English or Urdu
Session 1AI Awareness for Organizations
  • Generative AI capabilities and limitations
  • Business and departmental use cases
  • Workforce implications
  • Responsible AI principles

Practical exercise: Identify opportunities and risks in participant departments.

Session 2Departmental Opportunity Discovery
  • HR, finance, sales, marketing, operations and administration use cases
  • Task frequency and effort
  • Data sensitivity and readiness
  • Use-case prioritization

Practical exercise: Build a departmental opportunity shortlist.

Session 3Responsible Use and Governance
  • Privacy and confidential information
  • Output verification and human accountability
  • Bias and fairness awareness
  • Employee usage guidance

Session 4Adoption Roadmap and Action Planning
