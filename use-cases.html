AI Use Cases

Practical ways to use AI in everyday work

Seven business areas, with real tasks AI can help with, example prompts and the human checks that keep results accurate and safe. These are the kinds of workflows we teach and implement.

HR & Recruitment Marketing & Sales Finance & Accounting Administration & Operations Education & Learning Management & Leadership Entrepreneurs & Small Businesses

HR & Recruitment

  • Draft role profiles and job descriptions from approved requirements.
  • Prepare structured interview questions linked to job criteria.
  • Create onboarding schedules, checklists and welcome communications.
  • Draft HR FAQs from approved policy documents.
  • Prepare training plans and employee communication templates.

SAMPLE PROMPTAct as an HR documentation assistant. Using only the approved role requirements below, draft a job description with responsibilities, essential skills and selection criteria. Flag missing information; do not invent requirements.

Human review is essential for hiring, performance and other consequential employment decisions. Protect CVs, employee records and personal information.

Marketing & Sales

  • Develop campaign briefs and content calendars.
  • Draft social captions, email campaigns and product descriptions.
  • Prepare proposal outlines and sales communication drafts.
  • Generate audience-specific messaging variations.
  • Summarize approved campaign metrics and suggest test ideas.

SAMPLE PROMPTAct as a marketing strategist. Using the product facts and target audience supplied, prepare a campaign brief with objective, audience, key message, channels, content ideas and measures. Do not invent product claims.

Verify product claims, pricing, customer information, brand rights and marketing content before publication.

Finance & Accounting

  • Explain finance concepts using non-sensitive sample data.
  • Draft variance commentary from verified figures.
  • Summarize management reports and identify questions for review.
  • Create budget narrative drafts and scenario assumptions.
  • Prepare documentation checklists.

SAMPLE PROMPTUsing only the verified figures supplied, draft concise management commentary on the variance between actual and budget. Show the calculation logic and list questions requiring finance review.

Never rely on AI alone for calculations, tax, audit, investment or financial decisions. Validate formulas, source figures and professional judgments.

Administration & Operations

  • Draft SOPs and process checklists.
  • Prepare meeting agendas, minutes and action logs.
  • Summarize operational issues and categorize requests.
  • Create internal communication drafts.
  • Develop task plans and process improvement ideas.

SAMPLE PROMPTTurn the approved process notes below into a numbered SOP. Identify unclear steps and mark them as questions rather than guessing.

Check procedural accuracy and avoid exposing confidential operational or employee information.

Education & Learning

  • Develop lesson outlines and learning objectives.
  • Create practice questions and explanations.
  • Adapt explanations to different learner levels.
  • Draft feedback and revision plans.
  • Build learning resources from approved source material.

SAMPLE PROMPTCreate a lesson outline for the stated learner level and objective. Include explanation, demonstration, practice activity and a short knowledge check.

Review accuracy, age suitability, accessibility, copyright and learner privacy.

Management & Leadership

  • Prepare briefing notes and decision summaries.
  • Compare options and surface assumptions.
  • Draft strategic workshop agendas.
  • Summarize approved performance information.
  • Create action plans and communication drafts.

SAMPLE PROMPTPrepare a one-page decision brief from the information supplied: context, options, benefits, risks, assumptions and decisions required. Separate facts from assumptions.

AI can support analysis but should not replace leadership judgment or validated organizational data.

Entrepreneurs & Small Businesses

  • Draft business concept and value proposition options.
  • Prepare customer personas and research questions.
  • Create content plans and customer response templates.
  • Draft product descriptions and proposals.
  • Develop basic operating checklists.

SAMPLE PROMPTHelp structure a one-page business concept using the information below. Separate confirmed facts from assumptions and list the questions that need market validation.

Validate market assumptions, legal requirements, prices and customer promises before acting.
