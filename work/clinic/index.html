Appointments

Book your visit online

Pick a department and preferred time. Our reception will confirm your slot by SMS or WhatsApp.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking ahead reduces your waiting time.

Do you offer home sample collection?

Yes, within 10 km of the clinic. Book through the form or WhatsApp.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cash, debit/credit cards and mobile wallets.

In a medical emergency, go to the nearest hospital emergency department immediately. Online booking is not for emergencies.
