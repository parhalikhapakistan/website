Our Story

Three generations of fire and spice

What started as a small roadside grill in Burns Road is today one of Clifton's favourite family dining spots. Our recipes haven't changed: fresh meat delivered every morning, spices ground in-house and every dish cooked to order over live charcoal.

Whether it's a family dinner, a birthday or a corporate gathering, our team makes every visit feel like coming home.

— The Saffron & Coal Family
